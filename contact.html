Tell us what the joint has to do

Not which part you want — what it has to do. Torque, the space it has to fit, and how hard it will be worked. You get back a shortlist across every supplier we carry, with the reasoning, not a catalogue.

A person reads this. Expect a reply within one business day.

What you get back

  1. A shortlist, not a catalogue. Usually two or three parts across different suppliers, with what each one costs you in weight, length and backlash.
  2. The figures on one basis. Rated torque depends on bus voltage and thermal limit, and suppliers do not publish it the same way. We normalise before we compare.
  3. What we would not sell you. If the joint wants a harmonic drive and we would be selling you a planetary one, we will say so.
