Store/Find by spec

Every model, every supplier, one table

Sort on any column. A blank is a figure we do not have from the supplier — we would rather show the gap than fill it in.

On the "rated at" column. Rated torque is not a property of a motor; it is a property of a motor at a bus voltage and a thermal limit. Where a supplier states the condition we print it. Where the figure comes out of a part code rather than a datasheet we say so, and sorting will not rank it against a stated one.
